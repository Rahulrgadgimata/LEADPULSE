Three steps to a working list

The same pipeline whether you start from a target profile or a spreadsheet.

1

Describe your buyer

Industry, company size, geography and the job titles you sell to. Every search and every score is built from that profile.

2

It searches and crawls

Companies come from the open web, LinkedIn, Google Maps, news and job boards. Each one's site is then crawled for the pages that carry contact details.

3

You get contacts, scored

Email, phone, LinkedIn and a named decision maker where they exist — with a 0–100 fit score and a plain-language reason for it.

Already have the companies?

Upload an .xlsx or .csv and it fills in what the sheet is missing. Columns are detected automatically — one "Company" or "Website" column is all it needs.

✉

Ask for what you want

Type the fields in plain words — "owner name, email, phone, tech stack". Anything without a column of its own comes back beside the lead and in the CSV.

◎

It says what it could not find

Every row reports what was found and what was searched for and genuinely isn't published — so a blank never leaves you guessing whether anyone looked.

↓

Your sheet, filled in

Download the same list back as a CSV with the contact columns completed, and "not found" written where it is not.

Where the leads come from

Several sources run together and the results are deduplicated, so one company appears once however many places mentioned it.
